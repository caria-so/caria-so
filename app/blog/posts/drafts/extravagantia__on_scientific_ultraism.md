#### 1. Des Cartes and the middle class guinea pigs. 
It stands against the science that revels in materialism and the Aristotelian physics of the *yle*. 

A "scientific ultraism" — the science of the elites—combines correspondences, vertical causality, and divination, while reacting with a grimace to the intellect’s inhuman contortions before the lack of qualitative understanding of the phenomenon.
