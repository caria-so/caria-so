About me

Federico Caria

UX Researcher · Information Behaviour, Human–AI Interaction, Knowledge Graphs · ex-Baymard Institute · Marie Curie Fellow

Knowledge is never in the record. It lives in the unwritten — what an expert knows without thinking, what a teacher whispers to a student, why two things that look comparable are not.

I went looking for it in records. It isn’t there. Knowledge happens in use, in the meeting between a need and the tool that serves it, and the record is what that leaves behind. A residue.

That loss isn’t a big problem when someone who knows the field is doing the work — they supply what’s missing without noticing they’re supplying it. It becomes a big problem when the software has to make the decisions.
